### Changed

- An alert of the full bench (a red Saturday run of the short bench with judges or of a sequence, rule 1 of `docs/v2/banc.md`) no longer blocks a release (L903, D-39, Sylvain's decision of 26-11: replaying a red took 5 to 6 h, during which nothing else ran on cadence, and 2.0.19 was blocked). The release is allowed by the last green mechanical bench of the fingerprint (rule 4), and a red full-bench run after it no longer withdraws it; a red micro, mechanical or flash run after it still refuses (unchanged). The alert stays displayed until the replay of rule 2: a ⚠ line under the verdict of `cadence2 bench verifier-publication`, repeated by `cadence release` under the version line, in `bench statut`, the start-of-session hook and the lead's tour, with the benches to replay at the weekend full bench and their commands. The « samedi manqué » warning is unchanged. `bench statut` no longer reports a `complet` guard mode. Tests: `v2/test/garde-proportionnee-l730.test.ts` (alert + green mechanical → allowed with ⚠; alert without green mechanical → refused; red mechanical after the green one → refused), `test/release-banc-v2.test.ts`, `v2/test/anti-rechute.test.ts` (C5).
