### Added

- Bench in three levels (L689, D-3, [docs/v2/banc.md](docs/v2/banc.md#trois-niveaux-de-banc-l689)): `cadence2 bench micro` (bench 1, no AI judge, about 8 min) and `cadence2 bench mecanique` (benches 1, 3 and 6, no AI judge, about 30 min, after every change of v2) run the project suite and the original tests in each arm's clone instead of the judges; they never allow nor block a release. The full bench with judges (`court` and the sequence) stays the release guard.

### Changed

- The benches of a passage run in parallel (L689), each in its own `cadence2` process, three at a time by default (`--paralleles N`, `1` for the old one-after-the-other passage); the two arms of a bench stay one after the other. The report and the history line give the duration of the whole passage.
- The release guard measures `cadence run` against Claude alone (L689, Sylvain's decision of 2026-11-13): `bench` defaults to `--candidat cadence-run --reference A`, and `verifier-publication` only accepts a passage of `cadence-run` against `A`. A refusal gives the exact command to run from the repository being released (`node <repo>/v2/bin/cadence2.js bench court`), and every passage prints the repository, branch and commit it measures: on 2026-11-13 the bench had measured another clone through the `cadence2` of the PATH.
