### Fixed

- The lots panel (`/hud lots`) no longer says `Aucun lot non clos` when projects have open lots (L712, D-20, [docs/cadence-hud.md](docs/cadence-hud.md)): the tree of lots comes first in the panel's height. While it lacks the height that shows every lot in progress with its project, the bench, then the deliveries, the lots in progress and the decisions fold to one line, then give it up in the same order (the `⚠` line of a lot over budget stays, L414); the projects' order region also waits for that height. A panel too short to show one lot says `Pas la place de montrer un lot : agrandir le panneau.`; `Aucun lot non clos` only when no project has an open lot. The running bench line now counts in the height. `hooks/partage.ts`, `besoinArbre`; `tests/partage-l712.test.ts`, `tests/lots-pane.test.ts`.
- The lots panel (`/hud lots`) shows every lot in progress of every project before any other lot when it lacks height (L712, D-20, [docs/cadence-hud.md](docs/cadence-hud.md)): a project in progress placed 7th or 13th of `priority:` in a folder of 13 projects no longer vanishes from a 16 to 50 line panel. The tree keeps its first lines while they show every lot in progress; otherwise each lot in progress, with its project's name, comes first, then the folded `prêts` / `livrés` / `clos` lines of the projects shown, then the rest. The last line counts what is missing, `… +2 lots en cours non affichés · +N lots de plus` when even the lots in progress do not fit, and `Pas la place de montrer un lot` gives that count too. The height the tree asks for is computed on that cut (`besoinArbre`), no longer by trying heights on the assumption that a taller panel shows more. `hooks/lots.ts` (`buildPanel`, `horsVue`); `tests/partage-l712.test.ts`, `tests/lots-pane.test.ts`, `tests/lots.test.ts` (R9).
