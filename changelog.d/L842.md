### Added

- `cadence run` replays each red test of a failed gate alone before any correction (L842, D-33, [docs/v2/2026-11-09-cadence-run.md](docs/v2/2026-11-09-cadence-run.md#déroulé)): same command filtered on that test, declared by `orchestrate.testReplay` or deduced for vitest, jest, pytest, `dotnet test` and npm scripts. A test green alone is noted « sensible à la charge » (journal, end detail, `sensibles_charge` in the state), counts green and opens no correction pass; a test red alone is corrected as before. Without a filter (composed or unknown command, unnamed failure, more than 10 reds) nothing is replayed and the gate says why.
- `scripts/passes-porte-rouge.mjs`: share of « porte rouge » correction passes that end without a code commit, per project, from the run states (each correction now records its `commits` and `fichiers`), for a before/after comparison.

### Changed

- The readers of red tests and the replay helpers of `cadence orchestrate` moved from `src/orchestrate/baseline.ts` and `workers.ts` to `scripts/rouges-porte.mjs`, shared with cadence 2 (`v2`); behaviour unchanged.
