### Fixed

- CI recipe of `cadence analyse` (issue #72, L811): on the CI of a push to `main`, `--contre origin/main` compared each baseline to itself and let a violation committed with its baseline raised by hand pass. The recipe of [docs/analyse.md](docs/analyse.md#the-ci-recipe) now compares to the commit before the push, `github.event.before` (the base sha on a pull request, with fallbacks for a new ref and a manual run), and warns against `.cadence/analyse/**` in `paths-ignore`. The recipe is the protection: the command adds a guard on the last commit only, it refuses (code 1) a start that is HEAD itself when HEAD raises a baseline against its parent, and says a baseline born in HEAD without refusing it. A push of several commits where an earlier one raised the baseline still passes with `--contre origin/main`.
