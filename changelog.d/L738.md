### Fixed

- A bench's measured time noise now expires (L738, [docs/v2/banc.md](docs/v2/banc.md)): since L736 the two arms are no longer identical, so noise frozen on the older identical-arm history would have kept benches 3 and 6 out of the blocking time criterion forever. An identical pair counts for 14 days after its pass; a bench's noise is the largest gap among its still-valid pairs, so it expires 14 days after its last A-versus-A pair, and the bench falls back to the default threshold (1.5 × A total, 1.2 × A touch-ups), blocking again.

### Added

- `cadence2 bench bruit [--bancs 3,6]` (L738): an A-versus-A pass (A against its twin `A-bis`, same arm fingerprint), without judge, one bench after the other. The Saturday `bench complet` runs it first on every short-bench bench that is noisy or whose noise expires within 7 days (or already expired), to remeasure it. It writes its report for the noise computation but no line in the guard's history; the L730 guard and the L733 and L737 rules are unchanged.
