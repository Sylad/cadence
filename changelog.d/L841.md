### Added

- `raf demande new "<texte>"` creates the next entry of the request register (L841, issue 74 of the work account, [docs/raf.md](docs/raf.md)): `.cadence/demandes/D-<n+1>.json` with the text word for word, today's date, `etat` `ouverte`, `preuve` `null` and `precisions` `[]` — the shape of the entries written by hand — and prints `Noté D-<n> : …`. On a branch held by a program it waits in the pending plan like `raf add`, and its number is reserved at once (two `new` in a row get two numbers, pending or not; the entries a holding `cadence run` wrote in its worktree count too); applied later, the entry keeps the day it was asked. Until now the entry was written by hand and waited for the end of the run to be committed.
