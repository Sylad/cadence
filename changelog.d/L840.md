- A `cadence run` session may now run `cadence analyse` itself — without option, `--init`, `--contre <ref>`, `--outil <name>`, `--help` — instead of stopping on a question (L840): on 25-11, 5 adoption runs out of 6 saw `cadence analyse --init` and even `--help` refused. `Bash(cadence:*)` is replaced by a deny per tool except `analyse`, and the PreToolUse hook `v2/hooks/cadence-permise.mjs` (formerly `seul-spec-check.mjs`, now set in every run session) refuses `--ecrire`, `--migrer` (even behind a redirection such as `2>&1`, `>&2` or `&>/dev/null`), any other option and any other cadence or raf command. When the request or the plan's lot cites `cadence analyse`, the `analyse:` key or « analyse statique », [docs/analyse.md](docs/analyse.md#where-it-runs) is attached as a piece to read before writing.
