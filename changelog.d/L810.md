### Fixed

- `cadence ecriture` (L810, [docs/ecriture.md](docs/ecriture.md)): the negated passive with elision (« n'est pas livré ») is now flagged. A sentence no longer ends after an abbreviation (« Cf. », « M. », « Mme. », « p. ex. »…), after « Etc. » alone or inside a quotation « … ». An indented code block (4 spaces or a tab) is left out like a fenced one. The measure of the 20 News entries stays at 24.9 words per sentence.
- `raf news check`: an empty `ecriture:` key (no value, or all its keys commented out) now counts as declared and applies the defaults, as the documentation says.
