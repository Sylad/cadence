### Added

- `cadence2 bench mecanique` refuses to start (exit 2) while a `cadence run` is running or a lead session registered with `cadence lead hold` is live, and names each of them (run id, lot and repository; session label and expiry) (L762). Two mechanical benches were lost to the load the lead added on 16 and 17-11: the second was RED on time, 2269 s against 1490 s, with five `cadence run` in parallel. `--malgre-charge` forces the bench; the output, the report (« ⚠ Forcé malgré la charge » line), its JSON (`charge.malgre_charge`) and the history line (`malgre_charge: true`) say so. See [docs/v2/banc.md](docs/v2/banc.md).
- Every bench report now notes the machine load: load average at 1, 5 and 15 min at the start and at the end of the run, with the number of cores (`charge` in the JSON report) (L762).
