### Fixed

- `cadence spec reseal <delivered lot> … --porteur <lot>` no longer refuses a sheet amended by a lot dropped in the
  plan (L713, issue #56, [docs/orchestrate-spec.md](docs/orchestrate-spec.md)): a commit of the sheet whose lot is
  `dropped` or `done` is admitted, since a closed lot will not rewrite a pushed commit, and recorded in the `resceaux`
  entry under `amendements` (`sha`, `lot`, `etat`, `raison`) and in the body of the reseal commit. A commit of another
  lot in progress still refuses; the error now names that lot and the way out (`raf done <lot>`, `raf drop <lot>`, or
  `--porteur <lot>` when that lot carries the fix of the test). No new option.
- The refusal of `cadence spec reseal <delivered lot> … --porteur <lot>` for a sheet modified by a commit of a lot still
  `todo`, of no lot or of a lot absent from the plan no longer advises to « reseal by hand with the lead » (editing
  `acceptation-<lot>.json` bypasses every check, issue #56). It gives the tooled way out of each case (L713,
  [docs/orchestrate-spec.md](docs/orchestrate-spec.md)): a `todo` lot is closed (`raf done`, `raf drop`) or started and
  named with `--porteur`; a commit that cites no lot of the plan is rewritten to cite the carrier when it is not pushed,
  otherwise the sheet is put back to its red-commit content in a commit of the carrier, which counts as unchanged.
