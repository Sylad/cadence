### Fixed

- The journal of a `cadence run --fond` says who the run was and what stopped it (L711, issue #55 of the pro account, D-7, [docs/v2/2026-11-09-cadence-run.md](docs/v2/2026-11-09-cadence-run.md)): on 12-11 a run died at the end of the Claude Code session that launched it with nothing in its journal, and the cause could not be reproduced on Big-Blue. At launch the run writes its pid, pgid, sid, ppid and cgroup (only when `/proc/self/cgroup` exists); every SIGHUP, SIGTERM or SIGINT received by the run's group, by the wrapper of its `claude` session, or that stopped the run, is written with its time on a `cause d'arrêt` line, and so is the signal and code of a `claude` that died on a signal. `--liste` appends the last such line to an `interrompu` run. How a run stops is unchanged: `--fond` now detaches a watcher that launches the run, forwards nothing and writes what the synchronous run cannot; a signal sent to the watcher is written, then stops it as it would stop a process without a handler. A session stopped by its 4 h timeout is written `délai de 4 h dépassé`, and a SIGINT to its wrapper stays SIGINT (code 130), not SIGTERM. The docs say what to attach to an issue: the run's journal. `v2/test/run-signal-l711.test.ts` sends SIGTERM to a fake run and finds it in the journal and in `--liste`.
