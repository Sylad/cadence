### Fixed

- `cadence2 bench complet` builds `v2` itself when `v2/dist` is stale (a file of `v2/src` newer than `dist/cli.js`), then relaunches on the fresh dist; on 21-11 the Saturday bench had been refused for that reason, silently. Any full bench that does not measure (refusal, failed build, error, failed check of the systemd unit) writes its cause to `~/bench-cadence/complet-refus.txt`, and `cadence2 bench statut` shows it as a ⚠ line, so the startup hook and the lead's tour see it (L804).
