### Added

- `cadence ecriture` and the `ecriture:` key of `cadence.yaml` (L803, D-31, [docs/ecriture.md](docs/ecriture.md)): short writing, after the principles of ASD-STE100 transposed to French. The command reports, and never rewrites, each sentence of more than 25 words (20 for an instruction), each passive voice and each semicolon of the News entries (no path) or of the files given (review reports in `.md`, `.txt` or `.json`, folders walked). It ends with one summary line per group and gives the average number of words per sentence, the measure before and after. `--jour` and `--depuis` keep the entries of a day, `--resume` prints the summary only, `--json` serves a script such as the day's report of a parent folder, and `--strict` exits 1 when a signal remains. Thresholds and signals are set per project (`phrase`, `consigne`, `passif`, `pointVirgule`), checked by `cadence ecriture` and by the check of `cadence.yaml`.
- With `ecriture:` declared, `raf news check` adds a warning line `⚠ écriture : …` per signal of the entries dated `ecriture.depuis` or later; the warnings block nothing. The three reviewer agents (`code-reviewer`, `ux-reviewer`, `qa-reviewer`) write their report file under the same rule and run `cadence ecriture` on it before replying. cadence adopts the key from 2026-11-23: its 20 News entries averaged 24.9 words per sentence that day.
