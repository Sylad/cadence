### Added

- `language:` in `cadence.yaml` (`fr` or `en`, L791, issue #67): with `language: en`, the files cadence writes into the project are English. The accept step hands the English runner template `templates/acceptation-runner.en.mjs` (no French comment), the spec step the English sheet templates `templates/fiche.en.yaml` and `templates/fiche-back.en.yaml`, and the briefs of the spec writer and the acceptance writer ask for English rules, labels, test names and comments; `cadence spec check` and `cadence spec qa` then expect the states `empty`, `loading`, `error` (and `loaded`). Without the key nothing changes; the plan and the messages to the human keep their language ([docs/config.md](docs/config.md#language-of-the-project-files-language)).
