### Changed

- The "original tests green" criterion of the micro and mechanical benches (publication guard, L730) now counts the original tests each arm passes (`caches_reussis`, read from the jest, vitest and pytest summary lines of the two suites) instead of whole green benches, and is green when the candidate passes at least the reference minus a noise margin of 11 % (`MARGE_CACHES`), measured on 12 recorded pairs of identical arms on benches 1, 3 and 7 (largest gap 10.2 %). A candidate clearly worse than A stays RED; a run without counts falls back to the per-bench rule and says so. The report shows each arm's count (L777, [docs/v2/banc.md](docs/v2/banc.md)).
