### Added

- `cadence sondes <url>`: interaction probes for any web page (L778, D-14). For every checkbox, switch, toggle and list button it measures the jump of the clicked element after the click (0 px expected, a finding above 1 px, where Google's CLS ignores the 500 ms after an input), the changed region and its distance, the scroll the user did not make, the focus when a dialog opens and Escape closing it; it also watches the page scrolling by itself at rest. Journeys of a project are declared as `parcours:` lines in `docs/qa/expectations.md` and measured the same way. Read only, measured and stated: HTTP other than GET is refused in the browser's network for every target (page, iframes, popups, dedicated, shared, module and blob workers), WebSocket messages are cut in the page (Playwright routing) and in every worker (attached paused at its start, over the DevTools protocol), form posts, service workers, beacons, WebRTC and WebTransport are cut, each attempt is reported with the control that made it, and the report lists the workers it guarded; a worker it could not guard makes the pass « not verified ». Not covered: a GET that writes on the server side, a browser other than Chromium; risky controls are never pressed; checkboxes and switches are flipped back. Exit codes: 1 for findings only, 3 for a partial pass, 4 when no click was measured (« non mesurable »), 2 when the pass could not run; a button that navigates is reported as such, an element replaced by the click is followed by its equivalent or reported as not measured. Playwright is taken from the machine, never installed. The `ux-reviewer` and `qa-reviewer` agents run the probes at 1440 and 390 px and report their measures ([docs/sondes-interaction.md](docs/sondes-interaction.md)). On the live map of a project, the probes found by themselves the 140 px jump of a layer row that a review at rest had missed.
