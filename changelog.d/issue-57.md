### Fixed

- `cadence run` keeps the report its session gave when it finished its work (issue #57 of the pro account, [docs/v2/2026-11-09-cadence-run.md](docs/v2/2026-11-09-cadence-run.md)): a task notification that arrived after that report (a background agent or command finishing late) opened one more turn, and its reply replaced the report in the run's state and in the `fond-<id>.log` journal, so the commits, the sealed tests to reseal and the verified / not verified lines were only left in the session transcript. Sessions now talk `--output-format stream-json`: every turn's final message is read and kept, in order, in the state (`messages`), the report is the one that says « fini » outside a notification turn (`rapport`), and the journal prints it in full, followed by any later reply labelled as such. Test: `v2/test/run-rapport-57.test.ts`.
