A runner counts only when it has a summary in both logs and no failure in the arm's own suite (a `&&` chain stopped elsewhere used to count 1368 or 0); a runner counted for one arm only makes the bench "not measurable" for this criterion, which says so and falls back to the per-bench rule. A candidate clearly worse than A stays RED. Recomputed on the three mechanical runs of 18-11: red, red, green (L777, [docs/v2/banc.md](docs/v2/banc.md)).
