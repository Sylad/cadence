### Changed

- The "original tests green" criterion of the micro and mechanical benches no longer turns red for the model's draw on a case the reference itself largely fails (L905, D-39, [docs/v2/banc.md](docs/v2/banc.md)). On 26-11, two mechanical runs at the same fingerprint `731ad45d5f4c` were red on this criterion alone (81 against 84, then 80 against 81), all of it on bench 7, where both arms fail 52 to 55 of their original tests; A itself went from 20 to 17 there. Each arm now records its failed original tests per comparable runner (`caches_rates` in `resultat.json`); on each bench, the margin is 1 test per 6 original tests the reference fails on that bench (`RATES_PAR_TEST_DE_MARGE`, measured on identical A arms at bench 7: 16 to 24 passed over 12 runs), and it only forgives the gap of its own bench. A forgiven gap is reported apart, not blocking ("tests d'origine : écart dans le bruit de la référence"). One test lost on a bench A passes, a gap beyond the margin, or a record without failed counts still turns the run red. Tests: `v2/test/marge-caches-l905.test.ts`.
