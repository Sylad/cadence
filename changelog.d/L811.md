### Fixed

- `cadence analyse --contre` refuses a start that is HEAD itself when HEAD raises a baseline against its parent (issue #72, L811): on the CI of a push to `main`, `--contre origin/main` compared each baseline to itself and let a violation committed with its baseline raised by hand pass. A baseline born in HEAD is said, not refused. The CI recipe of [docs/analyse.md](docs/analyse.md#the-ci-recipe) now compares to `github.event.before` (the base sha on a pull request, with fallbacks for a new ref and a manual run) and warns against `.cadence/analyse/**` in `paths-ignore`.
