### Changed

- The `cadence-hud` band answers « can I close? » at a glance (L802, D-30, [docs/cadence-hud.md](docs/cadence-hud.md)): one line says `✓ rien ne tourne : tu peux fermer`, or `▶ tourne encore : 1 run · 2 agents …` naming what lives (this session while it works, live waves, live `cadence run`s, working subagents, background commands, unexpired lead sessions, the bench run). Only live runs keep their own line; finished ones (integrated, interrupted, waiting for a gesture) are grouped by state on one grey, indented history line under the answer. The agent counter counts the subagents that work or are held (`running`, `pending`, `waiting`: held by a background command, a plan's approval or a nested Agent call), not an `idle` one at rest waiting for a message. An expired hold that nothing alive carries is written grey as a forgotten reservation with its `cadence lead release` command, no longer as a red alert among the work in progress.

### Added

- `cadence lead plafond <n> | aucun` (L802, [docs/lead.md](docs/lead.md#lead-plafond)): the cap the lead applies under `CADENCE_MAX_SESSIONS` (never above it), written to `plafond-lead.json` of the cadence home. The free slots of the band, `orchestrate --status`, `lead tour` and `lead release` are counted on it and say the gap (`créneaux libres : 1 sur 3 (plafond du lead, réglage 5)`), and a wave launched without `--max-sessions` keeps under it. On 20-11 the band read `4 sur 5` while the lead held itself to 3.
